Rainfall in Bengaluru

From NASA POWER, 2018 to 2025. I need this because if a year has less rain the lake will look smaller, and that is not encroachment.

Average rainfall by month

Shaded part is January to April. All my satellite images are taken in this dry period so that every year can be compared fairly.
